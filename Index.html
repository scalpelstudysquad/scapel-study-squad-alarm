NEET PG • INICET • FMGE • UPSC CMS
🔋
Critical: Allow Background Activity
Without this, Android kills the alarm when screen is off
🤖 Android (Chrome)
1
Open your phone Settings app
2
Go to Apps (or App Management)
Settings → Apps → See All Apps
3
Find and tap Chrome
4
Tap Battery (or Battery & data saver)
Chrome → Battery
5
Select Unrestricted — this stops Android from killing Chrome in the background
6
Also check Notifications → SSS ALARM and make sure they are Allowed
🍎 iPhone (Safari)
1
Make sure you added app via Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen
2
Go to Settings → Screen Time → Always Allowed → add Safari
3
Keep app open in background — do not swipe it away from the app switcher
4
Enable Settings → Notifications → Safari → Allow Notifications
⚠️
Without Unrestricted battery on Android, alarms will be skipped when the screen turns off — no matter what the app does.
